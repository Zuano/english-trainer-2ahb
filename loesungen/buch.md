## S. 89 – Reported Speech

### Zeit-/Ortsangaben (Tabelle)
now→then · today→that day · here→there · this→that · these→those · ago→before · last week/month/year→the week/month/year before · yesterday→the day before · the day before yesterday→two days before · tomorrow→the next day/the following day · the day after tomorrow→in two days' time · next week/month/year→the following week/month/year

### Your Turn 1
1. Tom said to me (that) he would wait for me at the train station.
2. Pat asked me what I was going to do the next day.
3. Jason says (that) he wants to be a famous actor when he has grown up. (says = Present → kein Backshift)
4. Mr Smith told his secretary (that) she could phone from that office.
5. Pat said (that) he was living with his parents at that time, but (that) he hoped he would have his own flat soon.
6. Jill said (that) she usually took her dog for a walk after work. (Gewohnheit: auch „takes" erlaubt, Regel b)
7. He asked me if/whether I had ever met her before.
8. Mary said (that) the party the Saturday before had been great and (that) they had been dancing all night long.
9. Lucy said (that) in that village nothing ever happened. (Allgemeinaussage: auch „happens")
10. Tim said (that) he had missed his train, so he would be late for work that day.
11. The shop assistant said (that) the sales had already started the weekend before.
12. Father says to John (that) tomorrow they are going to meet Granny. (says → kein Backshift, tomorrow bleibt)
13. Sue asked Ann if she had liked her last holiday.
14. Mr Johnson asked if he might use his phone.
15. Father told mum (that) she did not have to water the flowers because he had already done it for her.
16. Paul said (that) he had just bought a new car.
