## 2 Practice – Dialogues 1–8 (Martins Lügen)

Daniella trifft Martin zum zweiten Mal. Zuerst die Zuordnung: Welche Aussage von letzter Woche (A–H) passt zu welchem Dialog? Dann die Antwort in der indirekten Rede. Backshift ist nötig, weil „you said / you told me / didn't you say" im Past steht; außerdem Pronomenwechsel I → you bzw. my → your, weil Daniella Martin direkt anspricht. „that" kann immer weggelassen werden.

**Dialogue 1** — Martin: "My boss gave me a promotion today!" → passt zu **H** "I have my own business."
**Lösung:** But I thought you said **(that) you had your own business**.
*Begründung: Present Simple have → Past Simple had; I → you, my → your.*

**Dialogue 2** — Martin: "I've never been to China." → passt zu **G** "I've travelled all over the world."
**Lösung:** But I thought you told me **(that) you had travelled all over the world**.
*Begründung: Present Perfect have travelled → Past Perfect had travelled.*

**Dialogue 3** — Martin: "I take the train to work every day." → passt zu **B** "I have a Mercedes."
**Lösung:** But didn't you say **(that) you had a Mercedes**?
*Begründung: have → had (Present → Past Simple).*

**Dialogue 4** — Martin: "Let's go for a beer tomorrow evening." → passt zu **E** "I don't drink alcohol."
**Lösung:** But you said **(that) you didn't drink alcohol**.
*Begründung: don't drink → didn't drink.*

**Dialogue 5** — Martin: "I was at a French restaurant. … the waiters refused to speak English!" → passt zu **A** "I can speak French fluently."
**Lösung:** But I thought you said **(that) you could speak French fluently**.
*Begründung: Modalverb can → could.*

**Dialogue 6** — Martin: "Sorry, I have to answer the phone. It's my flatmate." → passt zu **F** "I live alone."
**Lösung:** But you told me **(that) you lived alone**.
*Begründung: live → lived; tell braucht ein Objekt (you told ME).*

**Dialogue 7** — Martin: "I'm going to stay at home this summer." → passt zu **C** "I'm going to Miami this summer."
**Lösung:** But didn't you say **(that) you were going to Miami this summer**?
*Begründung: am going (Present Progressive) → were going (Past Progressive); „this summer" bleibt, weil es noch derselbe Sommer ist.*

**Dialogue 8** — Martin: "It was nice to see you again, Maria." → passt zu **D** "I won't forget your name."
**Lösung:** It's Daniella. I thought you said **(that) you wouldn't forget my name**.
*Begründung: won't → wouldn't; Pronomen your → my (aus Daniellas Sicht).*

## 2 Practice – Reported statements 1–6

Anweisung des Blattes: Zeitform NUR ändern, wo nötig („ONLY where necessary"). Entscheidend ist die Zeitangabe im Reported-Satz: Wurde sie verschoben (that week, that day) oder blieb sie gleich (tomorrow, next year)?

1. Joe said, "I'm going away this week." → Joe said he **was going away** that week.
   *„that week" = die Woche ist vorbei → Backshift: am going → was going.*

2. The athlete said, "I will win the race tomorrow." → The athlete said he **will win the race** tomorrow.
   *„tomorrow" bleibt erhalten → das Rennen ist immer noch morgen, Situation unverändert → KEIN Backshift.*

3. The news reporter said, "People have been protesting in the streets today." → The news reporter said people **had been protesting (in the streets)** that day.
   *„that day" → Vergangenes → Present Perfect Progressive → Past Perfect Progressive.*

4. The director said, "The company will continue to grow next year." → The director said the company **will continue to grow** next year.
   *„next year" bleibt → die Aussage gilt noch → KEIN Backshift.*

5. Last year, the doctor said, "You need to rest more." → Last year, the doctor told me I **needed to rest more**.
   *„Last year" → liegt lange zurück → Backshift: need → needed; Pronomen you → I.*

6. The president said, "I won't lose the election." → The president said he **wouldn't lose the election**, but he did.
   *„but he did" = die Situation hat sich geändert (er hat doch verloren) → Backshift: won't → wouldn't.*

## Merkhilfen aus den Regelblättern

- **Backshift-Leiter:** Present Simple → Past Simple · Present Progressive → Past Progressive · Past Simple → Past Perfect · Present Perfect → Past Perfect · will → would · can → could · may → might · going to: am/is/are → was/were.
- **Kein Backshift,** wenn der Einleitungssatz im Present/Present Perfect/Future steht, bei allgemeinen Fakten und wenn die Situation unverändert ist.
- **Zeit-/Ortsangaben:** now → then · today → that day · yesterday → the day before · tomorrow → the next/following day · last week → the previous week / the week before · next week → the following week · a year ago → the previous year · here → there · this/these → that/those.
- **Fragen:** Fragewort bleibt (where, why …), ohne Fragewort if/whether; Aussagesatz-Wortstellung, kein do/did.
- **Aufforderungen:** told/asked + Objekt + (not) + to-Infinitiv; Vorschläge: suggested + -ing.
- „that" darf weggelassen werden; Past Simple darf laut Linguahouse-Blatt auch unverändert bleiben (went oder had gone).
