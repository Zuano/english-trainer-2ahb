## Modal Verbs Grammar (PPTX) – Practice-Folie 17

(Lösungen laut Folie 18 der PPTX)

1. She **can** speak three languages fluently. → Funktion: Ability
2. When he was younger, he **could** run 10 miles without stopping. → Funktion: Past Ability
3. **May** I borrow your pen for a moment? → Funktion: Formal Permission
4. It looks cloudy today. It **might** rain later. → Funktion: Possibility
5. You **should** see a doctor about that cough. → Funktion: Advice / Recommendation
6. You **must** wear a seatbelt while driving. → Funktion: Obligation / Strong Necessity

## WS-Modals-2 – Grammar 11: Modals (Present and Future)

### Exercise 1 – Underline the correct word or phrase

a) **should** (I don't think you should tell anyone yet.)
b) **couldn't** (couldn't possibly = ausgeschlossen)
c) **can't** (Sicherheit: das kann nicht das Hotel sein)

### Exercise 3 – Satz umformen (Wort vorgegeben)

### Exercise 4 – Welcher Satz (A/B) bedeutet dasselbe?

a) **A** – We may know the answers tomorrow. (it's possible = may)
b) **B** – You'd better not ring him now. (Rat/Warnung)
c) **B** – You don't have to come if you don't want to. (needn't = don't have to)
d) **B** – You shouldn't work so hard. (Kritik/Rat)
e) **A** – These might be the keys. (perhaps = might)
f) **B** – We can't lock the cat in the house for a week. (it would be wrong = can't)
g) **A** – The decision might be announced next week. (possible = might)
h) **A** – Try as I may, I can never solve The Times crossword. (feste Wendung)
i) **B** – I know. We could go out to eat instead. (Vorschlag = could)

### Exercise 6 – Überflüssiges Wort streichen (✓ = Zeile korrekt)

## WS-Modals-2 – Grammar 12: Modals (Past)

### Exercise 1 – Underline the correct word or phrase

a) **can't have been** (Das kann nicht Nick gewesen sein – Sicherheit)
b) **might have given** (You might have given me a hand! = Vorwurf/Verärgerung)
c) **had to see** (Vergangenheit von must = had to)
d) **might have lost** (Vermutung: hat sich vielleicht verirrt)
e) **would have been** (es wäre unhöflich gewesen)
f) **could have been** (I don't know … = Unsicherheit)
g) **should have bought** (It was odd that you should have … – feste Wendung nach odd/strange)
h) **wouldn't** (they wouldn't go = sie weigerten sich)
i) **could have helped** (ich hätte helfen können)
j) **shouldn't have** (Kritik: du hättest nicht … sollen)

### Exercise 2 – Phrasen aus der Box einsetzen

⚠️ prüfen: Die Box ist im Scan nicht lesbar – Phrasen aus dem ALP-Standardschlüssel rekonstruiert.

a) You **shouldn't have** told her! (Kritik)
b) The plane is late. It **should have** landed by now. (Erwartung)
c) You **can't have** met my brother. I haven't got one! (unmöglich)
d) You **must have** left your keys on the bus. (logische Schlussfolgerung)
e) You **can't have** heard me right. I definitely said 204525.
f) The meat is a bit burnt. You **shouldn't have** cooked it for so long.
g) I'm sorry. I accept I **may have** been a little bit rude. (auch: might have)
h) You really **shouldn't have** taken so much trouble over me. (höflicher Dank)
i) Was it really necessary? You **didn't need to** tell the police, you know. (bare infinitive „tell" → didn't need to)
j) Keep your fingers crossed! The traffic warden **might not have** noticed the car's parking ticket has run out!

### Exercise 5 – Fehler korrigieren (manche Sätze korrekt)

a) ✗ → Surely you **can't have forgotten** already! (mustn't have gibt es für Sicherheit nicht)
b) ✓ korrekt
c) ✓ korrekt
d) ✓ korrekt (nach „funny/odd": should have)
e) ✗ → Harry **might/could have won** the match with a bit more effort. (may have = nur Unsicherheit, nicht „hätte gekonnt")
f) ✗ → You **might have told** me you had already eaten! (Vorwurf = might have)

## modals_extension (PPTX)

### Practise 1–14 (Multiple Choice; Markierungen in der PPTX = Lösungen)

1. **c) should** – My son should be home by now. (Erwartung)
2. **c) ought to** – You ought to go to the emergency room. (Rat)

4. **a) will** – I will call an ambulance! (spontane Entscheidung)
5. **c) needn't** – You needn't come too early. (nicht nötig)
6. **b) must** – Children must be accompanied by an adult. (Vorschrift)
7. **b) mustn't** – You mustn't talk during tests. (Verbot)
8. **c) must** – We must be near the fire. (Sicherheit/Schlussfolgerung)
9. **d) couldn't** – They couldn't hear him. (verneinte Fähigkeit, Vergangenheit)
10. **c) can't** – You can't be serious! (Sicherheit: kann nicht sein)

13. **b) May** – May I speak to the Chief Councellor, please? (formelle Erlaubnis)
