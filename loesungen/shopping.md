## 2. Quiz „Shopping Vocabulary“ – gelöst (alle 14 Fragen)

Antworten stimmen mit dem offiziellen Answer Key überein (1 B, 2 A, 3 D, 4 B, 5 C, 6 A, 7 C, 8 D, 9 D, 10 A, 11 B, 12 A, 13 D, 14 C).

1. Which of the following things can an **advertisement** do? → **b) tell you how much something costs** (Werbung informiert, z. B. über den Preis)
2. If something you bought was a **bargain**, it wasn't … → **a) expensive** (Schnäppchen = nicht teuer)
3. Which of the following things does a **cashier** usually do? → **d) take your money** (Kassier/in nimmt das Geld entgegen)
4. A book costs $5.99 and you pay $6. What should you get? → **b) change** (Wechselgeld)
5. Which of the following things can you do with a **debit card**? → **c) buy a bag of apples** (Bankomatkarte = bezahlen)
6. What do you do when you **exchange** a product? → **a) you replace it with another product** (umtauschen)
7. What do people usually do in a **fitting room**? → **c) they try on clothes** (Umkleidekabine → anprobieren)
8. What do you do when you **purchase** something? → **d) you buy it** (purchase = kaufen, formell)
9. When do people usually get a **receipt**? → **d) after they buy something** (Kassenbeleg nach dem Kauf)
10. Closest in meaning to **shoplift**? → **a) steal** (Ladendiebstahl begehen = stehlen)
11. What can you do with a **shopping cart**? → **b) you can put things in it** (Einkaufswagen)
12. What does **store hours** mean? → **a) the time when the store is open** (Öffnungszeiten)
13. An example of **weight**? → **d) all of the above** (Gramm, Kilo, Unzen = alles Gewichte)
14. When they go **window shopping**, people usually … → **c) buy nothing** (Schaufensterbummel = nichts kaufen)

## 3. Zusätzliche Gap-Übungen (eigene Kontextsätze, in der App)

**Shops and Services:**
1. If you want fresh salmon for dinner, go to the **fishmonger's**. (Lachs → Fischgeschäft)
2. I never buy fruit at the supermarket – I prefer the **greengrocer's** around the corner.
3. She bought a beautiful bunch of roses for her mum at the **florist**.
4. In Britain you can only buy wine and beer to take home at an **off licence**.
5. My eyesight has got worse, so I'm going to the **optician** to get new glasses.

**Shopping Habits:**
1. Before buying a new laptop, you should **shop around** and compare prices in different stores.
2. We didn't want to buy anything – we just went **window shopping** and looked at the beautiful shop windows.
3. I didn't plan to buy this T-shirt at all – it was a typical **impulse purchase**.
4. The fridge is empty, so I have to do the **grocery shopping** this afternoon.
5. The trousers didn't fit, so I had to **return** them to the store.
6. Many people take advantage of major shopping days like **Black Friday** in November.
