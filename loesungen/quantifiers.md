## Quantifiers (PPTX) – Annas Party (Folien 3/4, Lückentext)

Anna is planning a party for her friends. She bought **some** snacks and **a few** bottles of juice. She didn't buy **much** soda because **many** of her friends prefer water. There are also **a lot of** sandwiches, but there aren't **any** vegetarian options yet. She still has **a little** time to go to the supermarket.

Begründungen:
- **some** snacks → bejahter Satz
- **a few** bottles → zählbar, Plural, positiv („ein paar")
- didn't buy **much** soda → unzählbar + Verneinung
- **many** of her friends → zählbar
- **a lot of** sandwiches → bejahter Satz
- aren't **any** vegetarian options → Verneinung
- **a little** time → unzählbar, positiv („noch ein wenig Zeit")

## Quantifiers-Übung (= Key_Practice, 1–20)

a lot of, much, many, some, any, no, few, a few, little, a little

1. There aren't **many** honest people in the world these days. (people = zählbar, Verneinung)
2. This man is a mystery. We have very **little** information about his private life. (information = unzählbar)
3. She isn't the most popular person in the class. She has very **few** friends. (friends = zählbar, negativ gemeint)
4. Our team is losing 1:0 but there is still **some** hope that they'll turn the game around. (bejahter Satz)
5. **Few** students have such good marks. She is an unmatched genius. (= fast keine)
6. I've read only the first chapter that's why I don't have **much** to say about the characters. (Verneinung)
7. There is **no** cake left! Ben's eaten the whole thing! (no + Nomen = kein)
8. Can I ask you for **a little** patience? I'll be back in 5 minutes. (unzählbar, positiv)
9. Science doesn't interest me. It's the subject I know very **little** about. (unzählbar, negativ)
10. The party was ok but there wasn't **much** dancing. (unzählbar, Verneinung)
11. We'd better go to the petrol station. We've got very **little** petrol left. (unzählbar, negativ)
12. Tomorrow's party will be legendary! There'll be **a lot of** people! (bejaht + zählbar)
13. My wallet is empty. I have **no** money whatsoever. (no + Nomen)
14. In his new flat there is **little** furniture. Maybe one chair and a small table. (furniture = unzählbar!)
15. Her grades have improved greatly. She's made **a lot of** progress. (progress = unzählbar, bejaht)
16. Norway is a very rich country. There isn't **much** poverty. (unzählbar, Verneinung)
17. **Some** school subjects are easier than others. (bejahter Satz)
18. I could rely on him in the past but this time he offered me **no** help. (no + Nomen)
19. The suspect was released because too **little** evidence was presented. (evidence = unzählbar!)
20. There aren't **many** things to do in Egypt that are free of charge. (zählbar, Verneinung)

## Regel-Überblick (aus der PPTX)

| Quantifier | Nomen | Satzart | Bedeutung |
|---|---|---|---|
| much | unzählbar | Frage + Verneinung | viel |
| many | zählbar (Plural) | Frage + Verneinung | viele |
| a lot of | beides | bejahter Satz | viel(e) |
| some | beides | bejahter Satz (+ Angebote) | etwas / ein paar |
| any | beides | Frage + Verneinung | irgendwelche / (gar) kein(e) |
| no | beides | bejahter Satzbau | kein(e) |
| few | zählbar | – | (nur) wenige (negativ) |
| a few | zählbar | – | ein paar (positiv) |
| little | unzählbar | – | (nur) wenig (negativ) |
| a little | unzählbar | – | ein wenig (positiv) |

Typische unzählbare Nomen aus den Übungen: information, furniture, evidence, progress, money, poverty, petrol, patience, hope, help, time, water, milk.
